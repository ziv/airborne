Getting Started

2026-03-31

A development or more precisely, a remake of the 1989 F-15 II flight simulator by MicroProse.

The purpose of this blog is to accompany the project’s development, document the decisions made along the way, and capture lessons learned, among other things.

I began the project by selecting a technology stack and researching modern workflows with OpenGL.

For this project, I chose a language I was once familiar with, C++, and one I’m excited to dive back into. I’m also using the “raylib” library, which provides a solid foundation and saves me from reinventing a lot from scratch.

Let’s get started.
