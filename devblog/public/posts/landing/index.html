Landing

Landing is a tricky to implement and test. Come on, landing itself is tricky and require keeping wings leveled, nose up and not to fast. And all of that is hard to do in a simulator, where you have no real feedback from the aircraft, and you have to rely on instruments and visual cues.

In order to debug the landing process, I added a box to each landing area that represent the ideal landing spot. So its a great opportunity to show some screenshots of the landing areas and the boxes.
